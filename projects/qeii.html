All Projects Project 08

QEII

Applying skills learned from university to my part-time job.

Timeline Nov 2020 - Present
Role Lifeguard, Supervisor
Key Systems PID Water Treatment Controllers, Spa Pool Heat Loss Model, 3D Printed Lab Fixtures
Deliverables Thermal Loss Cost Analysis, Tuned Chlorine & pH PID Loops, Custom Test Tube Holder
  • Process Control
  • PID Tuning
  • Water Treatment
  • Instrumentation
  • Thermodynamics

During my time lifeguarding and supervising at Taiora QEII Recreation and Sports Centre, I have applied the engineering skills I learned at university. I look for opportunities to improve processes, remove inefficiencies, and make tools that help the whole team.

When we got a new cover for the spa pool, other lifeguards and supervisors didn't want to put it on every night. I set out to prove it saved enough money to be worth the effort. After calculating the heat loss with and without the cover, I found the opposite: the savings weren't significant, and once the time to put it on and take it off was included, the council would actually lose money.

Test tubes for the water testing equipment kept getting knocked over, so I 3D printed a holder to keep them upright, along with a stand for drying them after testing. I also helped tune the water treatment system's PID controllers, which dose the pools with chlorine and sodium bisulfate to control chlorine levels and pH but had been left on their default settings.

At QEII and other facilities I have also learned how to run large industrial water treatment systems, including backwashing filters, managing water quality and general maintenance. Backwashing flushes pool water back through the sand filters to remove the particles they have captured, and it is done when the pump pressures get high. Water quality is tested every 2 hours for free available chlorine, total chlorine, pH and TDS. I haven't changed anything on the filtration side; the filters are simply maintained and run as normal. Whether streamlining operational tasks or optimising chemical dosing systems, I apply an engineering mindset to analyse problems and put practical solutions in place.
