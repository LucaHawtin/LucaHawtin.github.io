All Projects Project 08

QEII

Applying skills learned from university to my part-time job.

During my time lifeguarding and supervising at Taiora QEII Recreation and Sports Centre, I applied the engineering skills I learned at university. For example, when we received a new cover for the spa pool, other lifeguards and supervisors were reluctant to put it on every night. I set out to prove it saved a significant amount of money so that covering it would be worth the effort. However, after calculating the heat loss with and without the cover, I proved the opposite: there were no significant savings, and once you factor in the time required to install and remove it, the council would actually lose money. Additionally, I 3D-printed a test tube holder for the water testing equipment to prevent lifeguards from knocking them over, and I helped tune the water treatment system's PID controllers. The system had been left on its default settings, yet it was responsible for dosing the pools with chlorine and sodium bisulfate to control chlorine levels and pH.

At QEII and other facilities, I also learnt how to control large industrial water processing techniques I have learnt how to back-wash filters, manage water quality and general maintenance. Whether streamlining operational tasks or optimizing chemical dosing systems, I consistently apply an engineering mindset to analyze problems and implement practical solutions. I actively look for opportunities to improve processes, eliminate inefficiencies, and create effective tools that benefit the entire team.
