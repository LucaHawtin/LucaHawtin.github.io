Wind Tunnel and Outreach Programs

Built and designed a wind tunnel for the KidsFest and Children's University outreach programs.

Timeline May 2026 - Jul 2026
Role Lead Designer & Developer
Key Systems Aerodynamics, Data Measurement, Modular Chassis
Deliverable Commissioned Wind Tunnel & Technical Docs
  • CAD
  • 3D Printing
  • Wood Manufacturing
  • Coding

I designed and documented a portable, modular wind tunnel for the University of Canterbury (UC) Human Powered Club, created as an interactive exhibit to introduce young minds to engineering. It was built for Christchurch's KidsFest and the Children's University outreach programmes.

It needed to be portable, simple to set up, and engaging for kids. I started in CAD, working out the rough shape and layout of the tunnel and splitting it into a modular three-section chassis so it could be carried to events and put together quickly. A car radiator fan provides the airflow, and a load cell measures the aerodynamic drag on each model in real time so the kids can compare their designs.

The first version was built for KidsFest, with members of the UC Human Powered Club helping with assembly. It was a working prototype that let us test the concept with real users. At KidsFest, kids sculpted plasticine vehicles and tested them in the tunnel to see who could make the most aerodynamic model. We also used the club's 2025 speed record trike to talk about how the same ideas apply to real vehicles.

Using what we learned at KidsFest, the tunnel was finished for the Children's University programme. I also fully documented the design so UC can keep using it for outreach for years to come.

As part of Children's University, we took the wind tunnel to Northcote School, a primary school. We ran the same activity as at KidsFest, with students sculpting plasticine vehicles and testing them in the tunnel, but adapted it to suit the different age group. We also brought the club's speed record trike along so the students could see a real human-powered vehicle up close.

Alongside the tunnel, we showed the kids UC's larger wind tunnel with smoke and lasers. I also helped at another KidsFest event for the UC Biomedical club.
