All Projects Project 03

Aluminium Assignment

Designed and built an aluminium structure to support a calculated load.

Timeline Feb 2026–Apr 2026
Team size 2 Students
Material 6061-T4 Aluminium
Outcome Successful Demonstration Day
  • Hand Calculations
  • SolidWorks
  • Aluminium Sheet
  • Manufacturing

The objective of this project was to design, optimise and fabricate an aluminium structure to support a specific target load with as little structural weight as possible. We focused on the structural stress analysis and the final assembly, managing our dimensional tolerances so the physical components met the design.

Before designing anything, we needed reliable material properties. We tested dog bone samples in a Hounsfield tensometer to get the properties of the 6061-T4 aluminium.

We then tested more samples to check our stress concentration calculations against experimental results, so we could trust the numbers in our design. Using the measured properties, we did hand calculations and optimisation to find the most efficient load paths. We designed against buckling and failure at the joints, aiming for a structure that would carry the target load with as little weight as possible.

We balanced the calculations with ease of manufacture, removing geometry that wasn't needed to keep fabrication simple and lower the risk of mistakes. We then made detailed drawings with the tolerances each part had to meet.

We cut and assembled the parts from aluminium sheet, controlling the dimensional tolerances so the finished structure matched the design. On the demonstration day the structure was loaded until it broke. It failed at the calculated force, which confirmed that our model matched how the real structure behaved.
