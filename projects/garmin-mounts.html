All Projects Project 06

Garmin Mounts

Designed aerodynamic and practical mounts for my garmin GPS device.

Timeline May 2025-Oct 2025
Ergonomics Flush Cockpit Alignment
Project Type Personal
Outcome Make useful mounts for use on my bikes
  • SolidWorks
  • Prototyping
  • Aerodynamics
  • 3D Printing

I designed and FDM 3D printed mounts for my Garmin GPS device to use on my bikes. The mounts were designed to be aerodynamic and practical, and to make the device easier to see while riding.

There weren't many mounting options for my Garmin on my bikes, so I decided to design my own. My first prototype for my time trial bike was an old Garmin mount held on with zip ties. When the zip ties eventually failed, I moved to a more permanent solution and modelled a mount in SolidWorks that would fit the bike exactly.

I 3D printed the mount and fitted it to the time trial bars. It sits the Garmin flush with the cockpit, so it is easy to see while riding and keeps the front of the bike clean aerodynamically. After the first printed mount worked well, I designed a second one for my mountain bike, made to be aerodynamic and practical for long endurance rides.
