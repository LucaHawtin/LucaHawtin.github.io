All Projects Project 05

UC Human Powered

Enhancing human-powered vehicle performance.

Timeline Jun 2026–Nov 2026
Status In Development
Project Type Club Project
Outcome Make improvements to past years vehicles
  • SolidWorks
  • Prototyping
  • Aerodynamics
  • Manufacture

This project is about modifying and updating past human-powered vehicles, specifically Maroro (land speed record bike) and Mako (trike). The aim is to make them faster and easier for smaller teams to operate.

Maroro was designed by the 2023 final year project team and Mako by the 2025 team. Both were known to be performing below their full potential, so improvements were needed.

Maroro is very hard to operate in practice, because a team of around 11 people is needed to launch and stop the bike safely. Our main goal is to design outrigger wheels so the rider can stop the bike by themselves, along with smaller changes to make it faster.

Mako needs smaller changes to make it easier and more predictable to steer. The project is still in development, with the improvements being designed and prototyped through to November 2026.
