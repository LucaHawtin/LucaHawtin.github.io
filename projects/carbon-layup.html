Carbon Lay-Up

Learning from and helping the 2025 final year project team with a carbon lay-up.

Timeline Jul 2025
Application Bike Monocoque
Project Type UC Final Year Project
Sector High-Performance Human Powered Vehicles
  • Carbon
  • Composites
  • Final Year Project
  • Manufacture

I worked with the 2025 final year project team to get hands-on experience in advanced composite manufacturing, helping with the carbon lay-up of their bike's monocoque. The team designed an aerodynamic shell around the rider. Seeing the CAD model of the shell and the rider position first helped me understand what the lay-up needed to achieve.

I helped lay unidirectional carbon fibre along the length of the bike's monocoque. The focus was on keeping the fibres precisely aligned and avoiding voids that would weaken the structure. This gave me hands-on experience with how high-performance aerodynamic shells are made, and showed me how a CAD model turns into a physical composite part.
