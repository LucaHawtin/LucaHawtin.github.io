Carbon Lay-Up

Learning from and helping the 2025 final year project team with a carbon lay-up.

Collaborated with the 2025 final year project team to gain hands-on experience in advanced composite manufacturing. Assisted in laying down unidirectional carbon fiber along the length of the bike's monocoque structure, ensuring precise alignment and minimizing structural voids.

This experience provided practical insights into the fabrication of high-performance aerodynamic shells, bridging the gap between CAD modeling and physical composite lay-up.
